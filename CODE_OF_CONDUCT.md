# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to every space the project uses: issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews.

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise the change, not the person who wrote it.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or publishing of anyone's
  private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project makes you uncomfortable, contact the
maintainers privately. Until the project has a dedicated contact address, use
GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or reach a maintainer
directly through their GitHub profile.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close
threads or block accounts that break these rules.
